Official Project Operations Manual

A Detailed Guide to Player Registration, Admin Approval, Live Bidding, and Real-Time Match Standings

TOURNAMENT EDITION: JSL 2.0 • FOR PUBLIC & ADMIN USE

1. Application Purpose & Overview

The Cricket Premier League (CPL) Master Portal is designed to manage local league cricket operations. It eliminates paperwork and manually tracked spreadsheet scorecards by providing a unified web interface for admins, franchise owners, and players.

Whether it is player registrations, fee validations, live franchise auctions with automatic budget limits, fixture schedules, or live scorer scoreboards, the app integrates everything in one clean portal.

2. Registration & Admin Verification Flow

The tournament entry starts with a structured registration and verification system. Both players and team franchises submit their profiles through public registration panels:

A. Player & Team Registration Flow

1
Submit Player Details
Players fill out their Name, Phone, Village, District, Batting/Bowling styles, and playing Category (Batsman, Bowler, Wicketkeeper, or All-rounder).
2
Photo Cropping & Upload
Upload a high-resolution portrait. An integrated square cropper ensures photos display in clean, uniform dimensions.
3
QR Code Payment & UPI Reference
Scan the official merchant QR code to transfer the registration fee, copy the 12-digit UPI transaction Reference Number, and submit the form.
4
Team Franchise Creation
Franchises upload their Team Name, Franchise Owner Name, Team Logo URL, and configure their bidding budget (default ₹8,000).

B. Verification States

Admin reviews each transaction in the control panel to authenticate the payment. The player's registration progresses through three distinct states:

PENDING Red State

Initial state. Shown in the admin approvals page. Waiting for the transaction reference to be validated.

APPROVED Green State

Once approved by the admin, the player is officially registered, added to the auction bidding pool, and public lists.

REJECTED Gray State

If the transaction reference is invalid or blank, the application is rejected. The entry is hidden from the auction pool.

C. Player Pass Download

Once a player's registration status is approved (Green State), they can download their official entry pass:

Official Player Pass Download

Approved players search for their profile on the public portal, click their name, and choose "Download Pass". This generates an official digital ticket card with their picture, registration serial number, and a stamp confirming they are active.

3. Dynamic Live Auction Engine

The live auction engine provides an interactive bidding platform. Admin controls placing players on the block, and the live status synchronizes instantly in real-time.

Auction Screen System Interface Flow

🏠 Sitting At Home Live Bidding Viewer

Spectators can open the portal on their smartphones or home PCs. The **Live Auction Panel** updates automatically in real-time as bids are placed by the admin, meaning fans do not need to be physically present at the auction hall.

🔄 Sold Player Reversion Option

If a player is sold to the wrong franchise by mistake, the admin panel features a **"Pull Back Player"** option. This returns the player to the bidding pool and reinstates the team's purse instantly.

4. Match Center Scheduler & Scoreboard Console

Matches are scheduled in the fixtures page, which tracks each delivery to keep points tables and player averages automatically up-to-date.

📅 Match Day Toss Picker

Admins schedule games days in advance without knowing the playing conditions. On match day, the scorer launches a setup modal to select the toss winner and first batting team before initiating scoring.

🏏 Active Scoreboard Control

Admins input runs, extras (wides, no-balls, legbyes, byes), striker strikes, bowler assignments, wickets, and innings swaps. Target scores, run rates, and balls remaining are calculated on each ball.

5. Public Spectator Hub & Standings Tables

Spectators are provided with fully transparent views of the league's statistics, points tables, and career rankings from the main landing page:

📊 Total Registrations Count

The portal shows the total approved, pending, and rejected players count on the main dashboard, letting everyone see how many entries have been validated.

📄 Complete Registered Player PDF

Any visitor can download the list of registered players as a PDF. Navigate to the **Lifetime Career Hub** tab and click the **"Export PDF"** button next to the search box to save the compiled list.

🏆 Points Standings Table

Tracks wins, losses, matches played, points, and Net Run Rate (NRR) in an IPL-style points table. The **top 4 positions** are highlighted to show qualification status.

⭐ Lifetime Career standings

A clean white table containing lifetime runs, wickets, batting average, bowling economy, and performance standings points. Features a name and phone number search bar.

🖨️ How to Export this Handbook as a PDF

Press Ctrl + P (Windows) or Cmd + P (Mac). In the print dialog, select "Save as PDF" as the destination, check the option for "Background Graphics" to retain the colors, and save.
